FAQ: Lyniro vs Dock

What is Dock used for?
Dock is a client workspace tool that gives customers a single branded URL where they find onboarding tasks, resources, contracts, and communication. It is excellent at the client-facing experience. It does not offer health scores, at-risk alerts, or email-first task delivery.
How does Lyniro differ from Dock?
Dock is built for the CSM's client-facing workflow. Lyniro is designed for the CS leader who needs portfolio visibility. The core differences: Lyniro delivers tasks to client inboxes (no login required), uses client-verified completion (not CSM self-reporting), and gives leaders real-time health scores across all accounts. Dock does none of these.
Is Dock or Lyniro better for SaaS CS teams?
It depends on your biggest problem. If clients complain about not knowing what to do next, or where everything lives, Dock solves that. If your CS leader cannot tell which accounts are at risk without calling their CSMs, Lyniro is designed for that. Many teams need both — which is why Lyniro is built as a complement to, not a replacement for, CRM and communication tools.
