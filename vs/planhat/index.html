Frequently asked questions

What is Planhat used for?
Planhat is a customer success and revenue operations platform used by CS teams that need a comprehensive view of customer health, usage data, revenue analytics, and full lifecycle management. It is designed for CS teams that have outgrown basic tools.
How does Lyniro differ from Planhat?
Planhat covers the full CS and revenue operations lifecycle. Lyniro is designed specifically for the onboarding phase — email-first task delivery, client-verified completion, and portfolio health scores. If onboarding completion rates and leader visibility are your primary problem, Lyniro is designed for that. If you need full revenue operations, Planhat is more comprehensive.
What is Planhat pricing?
Planhat does not publish pricing publicly. Based on market data, it is enterprise-priced similarly to ChurnZero and Vitally. Lyniro's founding beta is free, with planned launch pricing starting at $79/month.
