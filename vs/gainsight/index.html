Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Lyniro and Gainsight?
Gainsight is a comprehensive enterprise CS platform covering the full customer lifecycle — health scoring, renewal management, CRM integration, executive reporting. It starts at $15K+/year and requires significant implementation time. Lyniro is focused on the onboarding phase — email-first task delivery, client-verified completion, and portfolio health scores for CS leaders. Lyniro's founding beta is free. The tools serve different segments.
Is Gainsight worth it for mid-market SaaS?
Gainsight is generally over-engineered and over-priced for mid-market SaaS companies with CS teams under 10–15 CSMs. Most mid-market CS leaders spend the first year configuring Gainsight rather than using it. For teams wanting to improve onboarding visibility without a 6-month implementation, Lyniro is designed as a faster, more focused alternative.
What does Gainsight cost?
Gainsight does not publish pricing publicly. Based on market data, it typically starts around $15,000/year and scales to $50,000+/year for enterprise. Lyniro's planned launch pricing starts at $0 (free tier) and $79/month (Starter).
