Why most SaaS onboarding software fails CS leaders

SaaS onboarding software is a crowded category. Most tools solve the wrong problem — they improve the CSM's workflow without giving CS leaders the visibility they need to prevent churn.

The problem with most SaaS onboarding tools
  • × Clients have to log into a portal they barely remember
  • × CSMs self-report completion — leaders see optimistic data
  • × No health scores or at-risk signals across the portfolio
  • × Blocked tasks sit unresolved for days with no escalation
What Lyniro is designed to do differently
  • ✓ Tasks delivered to client inbox — no login required
  • ✓ Client verifies completion — not CSM self-certification
  • ✓ Health scores and at-risk alerts across portfolio
  • ✓ 48-hour block escalation built into the workflow
