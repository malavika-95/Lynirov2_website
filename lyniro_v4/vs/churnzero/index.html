Frequently asked questions

What is ChurnZero used for?
ChurnZero is a CS platform focused on automation — automated health scoring, playbook triggers, in-app messaging, and customer journey analytics. It is designed for CS teams wanting to automate routine touchpoints at scale. It does not offer email-first task delivery or client-verified completion for onboarding specifically.
How does Lyniro differ from ChurnZero?
Lyniro is focused on the onboarding phase: email-first task delivery, client-verified completion, and portfolio health scores for CS leaders. ChurnZero covers the full CS lifecycle with automation-first workflows. If onboarding completion rates and leader visibility are your primary problem, Lyniro is designed specifically for that.
What does ChurnZero cost?
ChurnZero does not publish pricing publicly. Based on market data, it typically starts around $12,000–$20,000/year. Lyniro's founding beta is free, with planned launch pricing starting at $79/month.
