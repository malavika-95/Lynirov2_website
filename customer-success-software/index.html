What makes Lyniro a revenue protection platform

Customer success software that surfaces at-risk accounts automatically — not at the quarterly review when it's too late to act.

📊

Health scores for every account

Composite health scores based on completion velocity, client engagement, and blocked task count. CS leaders see portfolio health at a glance — not buried in CRM notes.

📨

Email-first client engagement

Tasks delivered to the client's inbox. One click to complete. No portal login required. Clients actually engage because the friction is gone.

✅

Verified completion data

Completion is client-verified, not CSM-reported. Leaders see honest account status — not optimistic self-reporting that hides problems until renewal.

⚠️

Automatic at-risk detection

Accounts that have gone silent, stalled on blocked tasks, or fallen behind their go-live date surface automatically. No manual portfolio review needed.

🔒

Block escalation protocol

Blocked tasks escalate after 48 hours. The right person is notified. Onboarding doesn't stall silently for two weeks while nobody acts.

👥

Leader-level analytics

A portfolio view of all accounts with completion rates, health scores, at-risk flags, and TTV data — without requiring the CS leader to ask their team for status updates.
