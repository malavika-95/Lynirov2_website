The onboarding problem nobody
is solving correctly

Most CS teams manage onboarding across spreadsheets, email threads, and optimistic CRM notes. The signals are there. Nobody is watching them.

🔒

Clients won't log into another portal

Every onboarding tool asks clients to create an account, remember a password, and navigate something new. Most don't. Tasks pile up. Onboarding stalls silently.

Result: silent drop-off
👔

CS leaders can't see which accounts are at risk

CSMs know their own accounts. Leaders rely on what their team reports — which is subject to optimism bias. Healthy-looking accounts churn at renewal without warning.

Result: surprise churn
✅

"Complete" doesn't mean complete

When CSMs mark tasks done, nobody verifies it. The client never finished the training. The account looks 85% onboarded in the CRM. It isn't. Everyone finds out at renewal.

Result: fake completion

How Lyniro is designed to work

Designed for VP of Customer Success who need portfolio visibility — and for the CSMs and clients they manage, who need a frictionless way to complete onboarding.

1

CSM builds the onboarding plan

Set up stages, tasks, and owners in minutes. Assign tasks to specific client contacts by name — not just "the client."

2

Client completes tasks from their inbox

Each task lands in the client's email. One click to complete, one click to flag as blocked. No login. No portal. No friction.

3

Leaders see health across all accounts

Real-time health scores and at-risk alerts based on what clients actually did — not what CSMs reported.

Tasks that clients
actually complete

The biggest reason onboarding stalls is login friction. Clients receive a task notification, click the link, hit a login screen they don't remember, and never return.

Lyniro is designed to send tasks directly to the client's inbox. One click to confirm. No account required.

  • Email-first task delivery — no portal login for clients
  • One-click complete or block from within the email
  • Client-verified completion — CSMs cannot self-certify
  • Instant CSM notification on every client action
  • Automated follow-up after 10 days of silence

Three ways Lyniro is being
designed differently

These are the three design decisions that separate Lyniro from every other onboarding tool in this category.

📧

Email-first task delivery

Tasks go to the client's inbox. Completed with one click — no login, no portal, no context switch. Teams using email-first delivery see 40–60% higher completion rates.

Core differentiator
✅

Client-verified completion

When a CSM marks a task complete, Lyniro sends a verification to the client. If they confirm, it closes. If they dispute it, it reopens. CSMs cannot self-certify. Leaders see honest data.

Core differentiator
📊

Portfolio health scores for CS leaders

Every account gets a health score based on completion velocity, client engagement, and blocked task count. Leaders see which accounts need intervention — automatically, before clients escalate.

Core differentiator
🔒

Automatic blocked task escalation

When a task is blocked for 48+ hours, Lyniro is designed to escalate it automatically. The right person gets notified. Blocks don't sit unresolved for two weeks while onboarding quietly fails.

Why Lyniro exists
💡 Why Lyniro is being built

I watched 20 accounts churn at my last company. Healthy in the CRM. CSMs logging touchpoints. Tasks marked complete. Nobody noticed the clients had stopped showing up weeks earlier.

We kept seeing CS teams manage onboarding across spreadsheets, email follow-ups, and disconnected tools. The signals were always there — nobody was watching them in real time.

The problem wasn't the product. It wasn't the CSMs. The tools tracked what we did — not what clients actually did. By the time the signal was visible, the renewal was already lost.

Lyniro is being built to fix that. Tasks verified by clients, not CSMs. Health scores based on real client behaviour. Leaders who can see what's actually happening — before it becomes a renewal conversation they can't win.
