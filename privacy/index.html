Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lyniro ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our customer onboarding platform at app.lyniro.com and lyniro.com.

1. Who We Are

Lyniro is a B2B SaaS customer onboarding platform. Contact: hello@lyniro.com

2. Information We Collect

A. Information you provide directly:

  • Account information: name, work email address, company name
  • Plan and task data: customer names, contact details, onboarding notes
  • Payment information: processed by Stripe — we do not store card numbers

B. Information collected automatically:

  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, session duration
  • Device information: browser type, operating system
  • IP address (used for security and fraud prevention only)

C. Information from third parties:

  • CRM data you import (HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, Pipedrive)

— this data is processed on your behalf as a data processor

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and operate the Lyniro platform
  • Send transactional emails (task notifications, magic links)
  • Respond to support requests
  • Improve the product based on usage patterns
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do NOT:

  • Sell your data to third parties
  • Use your customer data for advertising
  • Share your data with competitors

4. Your Customers' Data

When you use Lyniro to onboard your customers, you are the "data controller" and we are the "data processor" for that data.

We process your customers' data only on your instructions. We do not contact your customers independently or use their data for any purpose other than operating the platform for you.

For enterprise customers requiring a Data Processing Agreement (DPA), email hello@lyniro.com.

5. Data Storage And Security

  • All data is stored in PostgreSQL databases hosted on AWS

infrastructure in the South Asia (Mumbai) region via Supabase

  • Data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3
  • Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256
  • Access is controlled by Row-Level Security at the database level
  • Each workspace's data is isolated from all other workspaces
  • We maintain daily automated backups retained for 7 days

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies only:

  • Authentication session cookie (required to keep you logged in)
  • Preference cookies (dark mode setting, etc.)

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

If you use our website (lyniro.com), we use analytics cookies (PostHog) to understand how visitors use the site. You can opt out by enabling "Do Not Track" in your browser.

7. Data Retention

  • Active account data: retained while your account is active
  • After account deletion: data deleted within 30 days
  • Backup retention: 7 days
  • Activity logs: retained for 12 months then automatically deleted

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Object to certain types of processing

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@lyniro.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Gdpr (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have additional rights under GDPR. Our legal basis for processing your data is:

  • Contract performance (to provide the service you signed up for)
  • Legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, product improvement)
  • Legal obligation (tax records, compliance requirements)

Our Data Protection contact: hello@lyniro.com

10. Children

Lyniro is a B2B platform intended for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

11. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or in-app notification. The "last updated" date at the top shows when changes were made.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights: Email: hello@lyniro.com Response time: within 30 business days
